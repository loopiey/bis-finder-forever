# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- New gear planner: see your whole set of gear with its stats and score. Click a slot to swap in another item and see
  what changes. Right-click a slot to put back what you wear.
- Best from my items: puts together the best set from what you wear and have in your bags and bank (set bonuses
  counted). Open your bank once so it knows what is in there.
- Try any item: alt-click it in Best items, or type /bis try followed by an item link, ID or name.
- Plan steps can be put in your own order with the arrows on each step (shift-click: to the top or bottom).
  "Sort by upgrade" puts them back in order.

## 1.2.0

- My plan: click a step (for example *Run Stratholme*) to fold or unfold it. Shift-click folds or unfolds every step.
- My plan: items are removed with the X button on the right of their line, not by clicking the item.
- My plan: crafted items show the skill needed, the reagents and where to get the recipe. Quest rewards show the quest
  level, who gives the quest, where they are, and which quests come first.
- Stratholme is split into two runs: Living (Balnazzar) and Undead (Baron Rivendare).
- The class bar no longer says "you" under your class.

## 1.1.0

- Minimap button: left-click opens Best items, right-click opens My plan. Clicking the open view again closes the window.
- Minimap button: stays visible when another addon (for example a minimap button bar) collects it.
- Shorter README.
