# BiSFinder

The best items for your talent tree, slot by slot, and a step-by-step plan to get them. For WoW Forever.

Type **/bis** or click the minimap button to open it. Left-click shows the best items, right-click your plan.

## Install

Install it from **CurseForge**, or download `BiSFinder-x.y.z.zip` from the **Releases** page and unzip it into
`World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`.

## What it does

- **Best items for every slot**, scored with stat weights for your talent tree. You can change the weights.
- **Any class and talent tree**, not only your own.
- **Now / Endgame:** the best items for your level, or for level 60.
- **Where each item comes from:** dungeon, raid, quest, crafting, reputation, vendor or PvP. Only items that are in
  the game right now are shown.
- **Set bonuses:** how many set pieces are worth wearing.
- **Upgrade plan:** pick the items you want and *My plan* lists the steps to get them, biggest upgrade first.
  Move the steps around if you want to do them in another order.
- **Gear planner:** swap items in and out and see your stats and score change. *Best from my items* finds the best
  set from what you have.

The first time you open it, it reads the items from the game. This takes a few minutes.

## Commands

- `/bis` - open or close the window
- `/bis gear` - open the gear planner
- `/bis try <item>` - try an item in the gear planner
- `/bis check <item>` - why an item is or is not shown
- `/bis minimap` - show or hide the minimap button
- `/bis rescan` - read all items from the game again

## Not counted

On-use and proc effects, resistances and bonuses like "+36 Attack Power against Undead" are not scored. They are
listed so you can judge them yourself.
